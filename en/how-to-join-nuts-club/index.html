Want to play poker at the NUTS club on PPPoker but not sure where to start? The whole process takes about five minutes: download the app, enter the club ID, send your User ID to the manager — and you are ready to sit down. Here is every step, without filler.

What you need before you start

  • A smartphone (iOS or Android) or a Windows PC
  • Stable internet
  • NUTS club ID: 111777
  • Telegram to reach the manager

New to the platform? Read our PPPoker overview first — it explains how private clubs work and how they differ from classic poker rooms.

Step 1. Download and install PPPoker

Get the official app from the App Store, Google Play, or the developer site (Windows). Create a login and password on first launch. No passport verification — a unique nickname and a strong password are enough.

Step 2. Close pop-ups and tap Join

On first login PPPoker shows banners and tips. Close them and tap Join in the main lobby to open the club entry form.

Step 3. Enter club ID 111777

In the Club ID field enter 111777 and tap Join. Your request goes to the NUTS admin. Approval usually takes a few minutes — sometimes instantly if a manager is online.

Important: enter exactly 111777 with no spaces. One wrong digit sends you to another club — or nowhere.

Step 4. Open Profile and copy your User ID

Open Profile (person icon in the bottom menu). Copy your User ID — the long number. The manager uses it to find your account and credit chips.

Step 5. Message the NUTS manager on Telegram

Send your User ID to @NUTSsup. Say you want to join NUTS and make a deposit. The manager will confirm your request and explain limits, tournaments, and bonuses.

Average reply time is under 10 minutes. Support runs 24/7.

Deposits and withdrawals

  1. Via manager — message @NUTSsup with amount and payment method (card, SBP, crypto).
  2. Via Telegram bot — open @NUTS111777_bot, choose Deposit or Withdraw, follow the prompts. Chips arrive in minutes.

Minimum deposit is usually from $10. Confirm rates with the manager — they depend on the payment method.

FAQ

Join request pending too long? Message the manager on Telegram with your User ID for manual approval.

Wrong club ID? Leave the wrong club in settings and rejoin with 111777.

No chips after transfer? Check payment details and User ID. A transfer screenshot speeds things up.

Can I play on PC? Yes — PPPoker for Windows uses the same join flow.
