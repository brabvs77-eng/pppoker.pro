Open Face Chinese Poker (OFC): Rules, Structure, and Scoring

Author: Roman Shaposhnikov

Date: 12.01.2026

Open Face Chinese Poker (OFC) is a dynamic strategic poker variant where players do not bet but compete to build three strongest poker combinations from 13 cards across three rows. The game blends math, intuition, and excitement — especially in Fantasy mode.

Goal: Build Three Strong Rows

Each player arranges 13 cards into three independent hands, obeying the rule that strength must go from strongest to weakest.

RowCardsStrength
Bottom5 cardsStrongest
Middle5 cardsWeaker than bottom
Top3 cardsWeaker than middle

⚠️ Foul: If order is broken (middle stronger than bottom), the player fouls and opponents take all points for the hand.

How a Hand Plays Out

1. Start: 5 cards dealt; place them across rows
2. Next rounds: 3 cards dealt; place 2 face up, discard 1
3. Repeat until all 13 cards are placed
4. Compare bottom vs bottom, middle vs middle, top vs top

Scoring

+1 for winning a row, -1 for losing; Scoop (win all three) = +3 bonus. Royalties for premium hands (quads, straight flush).

Fantasy Round

Qualify with QQ+ on top without fouling. In Fantasy you receive 14–17 cards and set all 13 before others. Re-enter with trips on top in Fantasy.

Hand Rankings

Bottom and middle use standard rankings. Top (3 cards): pair, trips, or high card only — no straights or flushes from three cards.

Why OFC Is Popular

Pure strategy without street betting; Fantasy adrenaline; decisions until the 13th card; you choose which cards to keep or discard.
