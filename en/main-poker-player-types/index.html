Who will you meet at PPPoker NUTS tables?

One key to winning in poker is understanding who sits across from you. Once you identify an opponent's style, you can adjust strategy, avoid mistakes, and extract maximum profit. Below are six common player types in PPPoker NUTS.

Architecture of Poker Styles

All players can be split on two axes: how often they play (Tight/Loose) and how they act in a hand (Passive/Aggressive).

1. 🧊 Tight Player

Behavior: plays few hands; prefers solid holdings; avoids unnecessary risk.
How to play: apply pressure with bets and raises; if they continue, they usually have a strong hand worth respecting.

2. 🗿 Nit (Tight Passive)

Traits: opens only premium hands (AA, KK, AKs); rarely bluffs; passive without the nuts.
Strategy: pressure with 3-bets; respect their raises from early positions (UTG) — range is usually top-tier.

3. 📞 Calling Station (Loose-Passive)

Traits: calls often, raises rarely; hates folding; chases any draw to showdown.
Strategy: bluffing is pointless; isolate with large preflop raises; bet big for value on all streets (Bet/Bet/Bet).

4. 🔪 Maniac (Extreme Aggressive)

Behavior: constantly bets and raises (NLH VPIP/PFR 40%+); creates chaos; bluffs in unexpected spots.
Exploit: wait for a strong hand (top pair, two pair); call down their aggression; hands like TT or KQs are monsters vs a maniac.

5. 🔘 Loose Player

Behavior: enters most pots (NLH VPIP > 20%) even with weak cards; plays out of position; can get lucky on turn or river.
Strategy: isolate with raises (large sizing) and value-bet strong hands — big pots vs this opponent are your profit source.

6. 🥸 TAG (Tight Aggressive)

Key facts: gold standard of poker (NLH VPIP 18–26%, PFR 14–22%); bets more than calls; pressures weak fields.
Tip: TAG is the best style for winning play — use it as your baseline.
