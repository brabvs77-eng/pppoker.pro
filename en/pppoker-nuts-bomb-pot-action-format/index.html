What is Bomb Pot?

Bomb Pot is a special cash-game format with no preflop betting — the small and big blinds are replaced by a shared ante.

Instead of blinds, all players automatically post a Bomb Pot ante, randomly set before each hand.

There is no preflop action: play starts on the flop, and the first to act is the player in the small blind (SB) position.

Double Board — Two Chances to Win

During a Bomb Pot hand, two full boards are dealt:

  • Two flops
  • Two turns
  • Two rivers

Each player uses their hole cards to make combinations on both boards. The pot is split in two:

Situation Who wins the pot
One player has the best hand on both boards That player takes the entire pot
Different players win on different boards Each gets half the pot

How to find Bomb Pot tables?

Look for tables with these icons in the lobby:

  • 💣 icon: marks Bomb Pot tables.
  • “DB” label: marks Double Board tables.
  • 1/1DB label: means Bomb Pot Double Board happens every hand.

Family Pot Guaranteed!

Bomb Pot guarantees huge pots from the start. Because every player sees the flop, this is called a “family pot”.

Preflop ranges disappear, giving way to pure postflop chaos where strategy changes dramatically:

  • Which line is better: value-bet all the way or bluff on both boards at once?
  • Can you pressure opponents when the pot is already massive?
  • Or will you crack under pressure and fold the best hand?
