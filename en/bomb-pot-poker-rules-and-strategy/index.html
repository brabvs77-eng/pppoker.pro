Bomb Pot is a unique poker format where familiar blinds are replaced by antes and play starts straight on the flop. This mode has long been popular live and is growing fast online. Club NUTS offers many Bomb Pot tables: from NLH to PLO and Mixed Game, with different limits and settings.

What is Bomb Pot

Antes instead of blinds — every player posts the same amount into the pot before the hand.

Play starts on the flop — after dealing, the dealer opens the flop immediately.

Lots of action — with a large pot from the start, straights, flushes, and full houses are common.

Bomb Pot appears in NLH, NLH 3-1, PLO, 6+, and Mixed Game. At Club NUTS they run permanently or every few hands.

Format features

1. Big pots — every hand plays for many blinds from the start.

2. Multiway pots — many players are always in the pot.

3. Unpredictability — opponent ranges are wider than in regular hands.

Bomb Pot strategy

Play strong hands
Do not overvalue top pair. A free flop increases the chance opponents make two pair, a set, or a straight. Bet only with truly strong hands.

Be careful out of position
Playing without position in a multiway pot is always hard. Avoid aggressive bets without good equity.

Watch bet dynamics
In Bomb Pot it is hard to read ranges on the flop. Focus on opponents' turn and river actions.

Double Board Bomb Pot
If the table uses a double board, play only when you have the nuts on at least one board. Medium hands on both boards rarely win.

Bankroll management
Bomb Pot greatly increases variance. Plan your bankroll to handle swings.
