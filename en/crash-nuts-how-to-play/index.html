CRASH is PPPoker's rocket game: place a bet, watch the multiplier climb, and cash out before the crash. At NUTS it runs 24/7. Here is the mechanics, exit strategy, and how not to burn your bankroll in one session.

How CRASH works

  1. Place a bet before launch
  2. Multiplier grows: ×1.2, ×3, ×10, ×50+
  3. Cash out before the rocket explodes
  4. 50% partial cashout — take half and let the rest fly for a second exit

Risk management

Cap CRASH at 5–10% of your daily bankroll. Set a target multiplier before launch (e.g. ×2). Do not chase ×50. Partial cashout at ×2–×3 is a solid default.
