Bankroll management survives variance when strategy alone cannot. Rules for cash and MTT at NUTS on PPPoker.

Cash: 20–30 buy-ins

Hold 20–30 full stacks at your limit. Move up at 30 buy-ins for the next level; move down below 20. No exceptions.

MTT: 100+ ABI

ABI = average buy-in of your schedule. $3 ABI → $300+ bankroll. See MTT guide and microstakes strategy.

Discipline

  • Session stop-loss: 3–4 buy-ins
  • Track results in a spreadsheet
  • Withdraw part of profits regularly
