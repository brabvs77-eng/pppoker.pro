Russian poker on the PPPoker platform is a specialized format developed taking into account the preferences of players from the CIS. Unlike classic hold’em, this discipline includes unique rules that are familiar to fans of “home” poker nights:

Game Features:

Seka: an exotic Asian card discipline

Seka (also known as Sak or Xec Xec) is a traditional card game common in Southeast Asia that has gained popularity on PPPoker due to the growing influence of Vietnamese and Thai players in the platform's ecosystem. This discipline is a unique hybrid of poker and local card traditions.

The essence of the game:

  • Uses a stripped down deck of 32 cards (7 to Ace)
  • Each player receives 3 hole cards
  • The goal is to collect the most valuable combination according to the local hierarchy: Trips (three cards of the same rank) - the strongest combination Straight - three consecutive cards of the same suit Pair - two cards of the same rank High card - if there are no combinations
  • Bets are made in a circle with the possibility of “all-in” at any stage

Gameplay Features:

  • High speed - average distribution takes 45-60 seconds
  • Simple rules, but a deep psychological component (bluff plays a key role)
  • Popular in Asian platform clubs, especially during evening hours local time
  • Average bankroll for a comfortable game: 50–100 buy-ins

Seca is ideal for players looking for a fast-paced alternative to Hold'em without the complex rules of Omaha. The NUTS club launches weekly seke tournaments with a prize pool of $2,000 on Saturdays at 20:00 Moscow time.

Crash game "Rocket": real-time excitement

Crash games are a relatively new gambling format that has gained popularity due to its simplicity and high volatility. On the PPPoker platform, in select clubs (including partner projects of the NUTS club), a crash game called “Rocket” is available - an analogue of the popular “Aviator” from Spribe.

Game mechanics:

  1. The player places a bet before the start of the round (minimum $0.10, maximum $1,000)
  2. The visualization starts: the rocket takes off, and the coefficient continuously increases (1.00x → 1.50x → 2.00x → …)
  3. At any time before the “crash” (random collapse of the odds), the player clicks “Take” and receives the winnings: bet × current odds
  4. If you don’t manage to collect it before the crash, your bet will be lost.

Strategic features:

  • The mathematical expectation is negative (typical for all gambling), but short-term volatility creates the illusion of “easy money”
  • Popular tactics: fixing at 1.50x–2.00x for frequent small wins or waiting for 5.00x+ for rare big wins
  • Auto-withdrawal function - the ability to pre-set the coefficient for automatic withdrawal of funds
  • History of the last 20 rounds is displayed in real time for analysis (though each round is independent)

Why “Rocket” is in demand in poker clubs:

  • Rest between poker sessions without the need for in-depth analysis
  • Instant results - one round takes 15-30 seconds
  • Social element: in the club chat, players discuss successful/unsuccessful fences in real time
  • Ability to place bets directly from the poker lobby without switching applications

⚠️ Risk Warning: Crash games are highly addictive due to the fast pace and variable reinforcement. It is recommended to set strict loss and time limits. Many clubs introduce mandatory breaks after 30 minutes of continuous crash play.
