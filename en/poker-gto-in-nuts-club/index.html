What is GTO?

GTO (Game Theory Optimal) is a decision framework built to make your strategy hard to exploit. You balance ranges and frequencies (bet / check / call / raise / fold) so opponents cannot print money by adjusting to your habits.

In solver terms, poker becomes an optimization problem: for each spot there are mathematically grounded strategies that maximize long-run EV when the opponent does not leak badly.

Brief history and context

Game theory met poker decades ago; modern solvers (PioSolver and others) made approximate equilibria practical. Online volume plus software pushed GTO from theory into everyday coaching.

How to study GTO — a practical path

1. Preflop charts — RFI, 3-bet, blind defense. Postflop GTO collapses without this base.
2. Think in ranges — yours and theirs, not one hero hand.
3. Simple solver spots — common flops and c-bet policies before multiway chaos.
4. Review — save hands, compare to solver output, adjust.
5. Add exploit later — deviate from baseline only when the leak is clear.

Blockers: a simple, powerful idea

A blocker is a card in your hand that reduces the chance villain holds a specific strong combo. Example: you hold A♣K♥ on J♣9♣8♦ — the A♣ blocks some nut-flush combinations.

Blockers help you bluff more confidently, choose better sizes, and keep bluff/value mixes coherent. They are core to modern postflop GTO thinking.

GTO vs exploit — how to combine them

GTO is your safe baseline — hard to punish systematically.

Exploit means adjusting to a specific leak (for example: they overfold to 3-bets → widen your bluffs).

Strong players keep a solid GTO core and deviate when the opponent is clearly wrong. Pure exploit without a baseline makes you fragile against better regs.

Should you study GTO?

Yes — not as dogma, but as a map. GTO study improves range vision, shows how far you can lean into exploit, and raises long-run win rate when applied with discipline.

Start with preflop and simple postflop nodes; expand when those feel automatic.

Practice at the NUTS club

The NUTS club on PPPoker is a practical lab: fast cash, SPIN and MTT grids, and side games (including Bomb Pot) where range balance meets unusual textures.

Train at low and mid stakes, save hands for solver or coach review, and use NUTS side formats for multiway, short-deck, and other edge cases.

Conclusion

GTO is a tool: a mathematical base for unexploitable strategy plus room for calculated exploits. Study, review, and apply — NUTS is a strong arena for that loop.
