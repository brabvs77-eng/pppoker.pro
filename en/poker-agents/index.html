Who agents and affiliates are

In poker, agents (or affiliates) are usually partners of clubs and poker rooms who help attract new players. In return they receive compensation — most often a share of rake or bonuses from the activity of referred users.

In the PPPoker model, where the NUTS club operates, an ID invitation system is used. A player enters a special code when registering, and the club representative can track their results and award bonuses.

The agent's role

A club partner has several important tasks:

  • helping newcomers with registration and explaining how to join the club correctly;
  • assisting with deposits and withdrawals, especially when there are nuances with payment systems;
  • informing about bonuses, promotions, rakeback, and special terms;
  • maintaining contact between the player and administration, which helps resolve issues faster.

Benefits for players

✅ Main advantages

  • Easy start. It is easier to understand registration and the first steps.
  • Better terms. Agents often provide extra rakeback or exclusive bonuses.
  • Financial help. Deposits and cash-outs can be processed much faster.
  • Fast support. An experienced agent knows how to handle disputed situations.

⚠️ What to watch out for

  • Check the agent's reputation.
  • Clarify terms in advance: rakeback size, possible fees.
  • Understand how withdrawals work — sometimes the process can take longer.
  • Never share your account passwords.

Affiliate vs agent: what's the difference

Although the concepts are close, there are nuances:

  • Affiliate is most often limited to attracting players.
  • Agent regularly stays in touch with the player: helps with deposits and answers questions.

You could say an affiliate is a more "passive" role, while an agent is more "active".

Summary

A poker agent is not a mandatory part of the process, but playing with one often becomes easier and more profitable. The player gets convenience and bonuses, and the club partner gets rewarded for their work.
