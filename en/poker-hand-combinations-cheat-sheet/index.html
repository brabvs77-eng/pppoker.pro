In Texas Hold'em the best five-card hand wins. You get 2 hole cards and 5 board cards — pick any 5. Full ranking from weakest to strongest.

Hand rankings

  1. High card
  2. Pair
  3. Two pair
  4. Three of a kind (set)
  5. Straight
  6. Flush
  7. Full house
  8. Four of a kind (quads)
  9. Straight flush
  10. Royal flush

Kickers

When combinations tie, compare kickers — the highest side cards. See Texas Hold'em rules and starting hands.
