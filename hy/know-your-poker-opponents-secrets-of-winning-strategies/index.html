Hodvatsi heghinak: PPPoker

Onlayn poker@ lits e strategianer ev psixologakan paterazmi: Tarber tesaki pokeri khaghacoghneri haskanal ev nakhagitsum@ hachaxut'yan bnut'yun e: Amena khaghacoghi uslub@ azdum e nranq iranc vartman ev vachar qabulman gortsichn vra:

Sa hodvac@ kazmum e hing taracum tesaki pokeri khaghacoghner ev aynpisi terminneri lutsman@, inch en: Calling Station (avtomat patasxanich), Maniac (man'yak), Nit (nit), TAG (payt-agresiv) ev LAG (luz-agresiv):

Sa gitut'yunn@ karogh e ognel dzez pokeri khagherum ev mrcasharnerum, vortegh mrcakitsneri tana@ kafolatvum e hachaxut'yun bolor frontnerum:

1. Avtomat patasxanich

Nakhagitsner:

Avtomat patasxanich@ shat pasiv, bayc nuyn zhamanak aktiv tesaki khaghacogh e: Nranq khaghum en shat razdach flopeic heto, shtap ogtagorcum en kol, ays p'ox@ reyz: Nranq dzern ughi haskanum@ nisbakan t'al e, aynuhet nranq hachax kollum en t'al dzernov:

Strategia:

Avtomat patasxanichi dem strategia@ naevl e aveli shat reyz anel arzheq@ maksimalacnelu hamar: Nranq t'al dzernov koll anelu skzbunq@ hashvi arnelov, k'ez reyz@ dzernov kareli e znachitel zavelacnel dzer stek@ zhamanakn antar:

2. Man'yak

Nakhagitsner:

Man'yak@ aynpisi agresiv khaghacogh e, vor khaghum e shat razdach flopeic heto ev hachax bet, reyz ev dahe 4-bet e anum: Ays khaghi uslub@ berum e barcr dispersia nranq ardyunqnerum:

Strategia:

Man'yaki dem khaghum amenakarevor@ naevl e pahpanel k'ez k'ez ev spasel lav hnaravorutyunner: Nranq hachax t'al dzernov bet en anum, dzez kareli e nranq dem dzernov khaghali: Shat karevor e ch'pataharvel nranq agresiv uslubin ev ch'k'ezanvel: Pahpanir dzer strategian ev spasir hajogh zharbakman:

3. Nit

Nakhagitsner:

Nit@ shat konservativ tesaki khaghacogh e: Nranq shat kich razdach en khaghum flopeic heto ev shtap miayn dzernov khaghum en, erb unen kareli kart: Nranq khaghi@ shat payt e, ev nranq hachax unen lits stek:

Strategia:

Nit-eri dem strategia@ naevl e ogtagorcel nranq konservativizm@, k'azmel blayndner ev poqr banker: Dzez kareli e aveli shat ogtagorcel stil strategianer, vorovhetev nranq aveli shat e, vor kart@ k'azmen: Bayc, erb nranq banki nkatmambar zgushac'um en, zgushac'ir, vorovhetev nranq, skoree vsego, kareli dzern unen:

4. Payt – agresiv

Nakhagitsner:

Payt-agresiv khaghacoghner@ nakhaghecnum en khaghin ev agresiv khaghum: Nranq kich razdach en khaghum flopeic heto, bayc kareli reyz anum: Shtap nranq khaghin bacum en payt dzernov ev hachax unen lav stek:

Strategia:

Payt-agresiv khaghacoghner@ hachax shat kareli mrcakitsner en iranc mshakum khaghi patcharov: Nranq dem strategia@ naevl e ogtagorcel pozicion avantaj ev nakhaghecnel nranq betayin naxagitsner@: P'ordzel haskanal nranq t'al koxmer@ ev xnel anhajogh risker, erb nranq oshkora uzum en nayev:

5. Luz – agresiv

Nakhagitsner:

Luz-agresiv khaghacoghner@ shat razdach en khaghum flopeic heto, payt-agresivneri nisbatan aveli luz diapazonov: Nranq denc agresiv en, hachax 3-bet en anum, bayc ch'aynpisi ekstremal, inch man'yakner@:

Strategia:

Luz-agresiv khaghacoghneri dem strategia@ naevl e nranq agresian dem khaghali: Dzez kareli e aveli shat kol anel ev jamanaki reyz, vorovhetev nranq kareli e t'al dzernov bet anen: Karevor e mnal sabrakan, spasel hajogh zharbakman ev maksimalacnel arzheq@, erb uneq kareli dzern:

Yezrakatsut'yun

Tarber tesaki pokeri khaghacoghneri haskanal ev nakhagitsum@ karogh e ognel razrabotel aveli ardyunavet strategianer ev bardzracnel dzer hachaxut'yan tokos@: Amena tesaki khaghacoghner@ unen iranc unikal vartman naxagitsner ev t'al koxmer: Ays teghekatvut'yan gibk ogtagorcum@ kazmum e dzez avantaj pokeri seghani mot:
